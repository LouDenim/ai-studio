How it works

From your product to your video, in five steps.

  1. 1You tell me about your product

    A brief, a call, or a simple message: what you sell, who it’s for, what you want people to do.

  2. 2We lock the concept together

    I come back with a direction and a clear quote. You know exactly what you get before anything is produced.

  3. 3I create the video

    The faces, the setting, the light, the sound: everything made to measure for your brand.

  4. 4You react, I refine

    You get a first cut to comment on. Two rounds of revisions are included in every price.

  5. 5You get the final files

    Edited, colour-graded, exported in the format your platform needs.
