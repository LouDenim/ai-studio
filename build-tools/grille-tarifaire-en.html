Terms

All prices exclude VAT — applicable VAT is added to the quote.

2 revisions included in every project — beyond that, billed per the rates above. · Standard delivery: 7 to 10 business days depending on the project.

An approved storyboard sets the production starting point: any change to an already-approved element (storyboard, script, shot) counts as a billable revision round.

Source files, prompts and workflows not delivered — property of Lou Denim.

The rates in this card are a basis for estimation. The final quote is set by Lou Denim. Production costs may be added depending on the complexity of the project and the volume of generations required.

Web & social-media usage rights: included. Any other distribution requires a separate licence: press, print (catalogue, brochure), digital campaign, TV, cinema, 4×3 out-of-home, in-store screens. Territory and duration to be agreed with Lou Denim.

50% deposit above £1,000, required before starting. · No work begins without a validated brief and a signed agreement.
